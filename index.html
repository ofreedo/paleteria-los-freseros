Bienvenidos

Bienvenidos a Los Freseros

Tucked along Highway 9 in Ben Lomond, Los Freseros brings the flavors of a Mexican paletería straight to the Santa Cruz Mountains — fresh fruit, real cream, chamoy, and Tajín, made the way it's supposed to be made.

Every paleta, nieve, and mangonada is built for the craving you actually have: something sweet, something tropical, something with a little chile kick. This is a neighborhood spot first — family-run, fruit-forward, and proud of where it comes from.
